Friendship & Drama: The Real School Life

Because it's not always picture perfect.

Chidinma Onyeka April 06, 2026 7 min read
Ad
Chidinma Onyeka
Class: JSS2

Okay so I wrote this at 11pm after a Very Eventful Week. You'll understand.

first of all

School without your people? Literally unbearable. Like what are you even doing there?? You need that one person (or that group chat) that makes everything survivable.

And when you find them? It's everything. You're sharing snacks, gisting about literally nothing, laughing at stuff that wouldn't be funny to anyone else. That's the best version of school.

the lunch table, the back of the class, the random corner you always end up in — that's your whole world for a while.

and then...

Someone starts acting different. You're not sure when it changed but it did. Messages left on read. Weird energy in the group. People choosing sides over something that didn't even need sides.

And you're just standing there like... wait, how did we get here? We were FINE like two weeks ago??

Peer pressure starts creeping in. People start testing each other's loyalty. Drama that started as something small becomes a whole Situation.

the thing nobody wants to admit

Some friendships aren't built to last. And that HURTS. It genuinely does. Especially when you thought these were your people forever.

But here's what I've figured out: staying in something draining isn't loyalty. It's just exhausting yourself for free.

choosing peace isn't soft. it's actually really hard. but it's worth it.

real ones tho

The friendships that are real — you know because you don't have to perform in them. No proving yourself, no walking on eggshells. Just actually existing around each other.

Those are the ones to protect. Not every friendship needs to be kept. But the good ones? Hold on tight fr.
