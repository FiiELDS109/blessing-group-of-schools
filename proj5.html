Motion and Visual Effects: The Post-Production Pipeline

Turning raw footage into compelling narratives that move, inform, and inspire

Video Editing Team Lead: Precious Ikubiwe Supervisor: Mrs. Grace Achudume
Ad

Professional video editing is a technical craft built on a solid understanding of how digital video actually works. The BGS Video Editing track starts at the fundamentals — codec compression, frame rates, colour spaces, and file formats — because students who understand the underlying technology make better creative decisions and spend less time working around problems they don't understand.

Students work primarily in CapCut or any of the "X"-Cut derivatives and later get introduced to DaVinci Resolve, the industry-standard platform for professional editing and colour grading, learning to navigate its edit, colour, and Fusion pages with growing confidence. They work in log and RAW formats, applying LUTs, and building grade structures that give footages a consistent, intentional look across an entire project rather than a polished clip in isolation.

The curriculum extends into motion graphics and visual effects, introducing students to compositing principles, keyframe animation, motion tracking, and the use of expressions to build scalable assets. These are no longer niche skills — they are expected across the production industry, from short-form social content to broadcast and long-form documentary work, and students are trained accordingly.

Students of this track leave with a portfolio demonstrating genuine proficiency across the full post-production pipeline. Their fluency in CapCut & DaVinci Resolve, combined with working knowledge of motion graphics and compositing, positions them well for further study or entry-level work in film production, broadcast media, and the growing field of interactive and immersive content.
