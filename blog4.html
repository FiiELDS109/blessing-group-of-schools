Dream Big, Start Small

YOUR FUTURE STARTS NOW.

David Idise April 11, 2026 6 min read
Ad
David Idise
Class: JSS3

I know this sounds like a motivational poster. Just read it first and then judge me. Lol.

THE QUESTION THAT HITS DIFFERENT

Somebody asks "what do you want to be?" and honestly? The honest answer half the time is "I don't know yet" but you can't say that or everyone looks at you funny.

So you say something safe. Something that sounds acceptable. And inside you're like... is that actually what I want?

the dream is in there. it's just scared.

THE FEAR PART

Fear is loud when it comes to this stuff. What if I try and I'm not good enough? What if people laugh? What if I spend years on something and it doesn't work out?

Those questions are real. I'm not going to tell you to just "ignore fear" because that's not how it works.

But here's what I've noticed: fear is loudest right before you start. The moment you actually begin? It gets quieter

THE START IS NEVER PRETTY

Every person you look up to right now started from absolute zero. Confused, probably failing at something, figuring it out as they went.

They didn't wait until they were ready. They got ready by doing it.

start before you feel ready. that's the whole secret.

AND THEN JUST KEEP GOING

Dreams are built in small moments. The day you try the thing. The week you stick with it even when it's hard. The month you look back and realize how far you've come.

You don't need a perfect plan. You need a first step and enough stubbornness to keep taking the next one.

Start now. Build as you go. Your future is literally being made by what you do today
