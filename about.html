A Word from the Valedictorian
Chiderah Anene
Valedictorian, Class of 2026 · Best Graduating Student

When I first walked through the gates of Blessing Group of Schools, I was thirteen years old, carrying a second-hand backpack and a fear so heavy I could barely breathe. I did not know anyone. I did not know the rules. I did not even know if I was smart enough to be here.

But BGS taught me something that no textbook could contain: the classroom is not just a room with four walls and a blackboard. It is a training ground for the person you will become. Every teacher who pushed us harder than we thought we could go, every test that humbled us, every group project that taught us how to listen, lead, and disagree respectfully — all of it was preparation.

I remember the day I almost gave up on my BECE Exams in JSS3. I had failed three consecutive preparatory assignments. I was ready to tell my teacher I wanted to quit. And she looked at me and said something I will carry for the rest of my life: "Failure is not the opposite of success. It is the path to it." That rocky experience of then has became the foundation of why I personally want to become an inspiration to my juniors just as my teachers were, are and will always be to me.

This alumni network is not just a website. It is a living, breathing community of people who all once shared the same corridors, the same school anthem, the same experience of becoming. When you read a project by a current student, or a blog by a graduate who went on to do remarkable things, you are reading your own story — because we are all connected by the same beginning.

To every current student: do not be in a hurry. BGS is giving you something that will outlast any certificate. To every alumnus: we have a responsibility. This platform exists so that we can give back what was so generously given to us. Once blessed, always family.

Chiderah Anene — Valedictorian, BGS Class of 2026 · Aspiring to study Medicine & Surgery.
A Message from the Principal
Mrs Marian Ofumaduadike
Principal, Blessing Group of Schools · Since 2003

When we founded Blessing Group of Schools twenty-five years ago, we had one simple vision: to create a place where every child, regardless of background or circumstance, would be seen, nurtured, and equipped to change their world.

Twenty-five years later, I look at what has been built — not in brick and mortar, but in lives — and I am overwhelmed with gratitude. Our alumni are doctors, engineers, lawyers, farmers, entrepreneurs, artists, and leaders. They are raising the next generation, solving community problems, and carrying the name of this school to places we never imagined.

This alumni platform is the fulfilment of something I have always believed: that the relationship between a school and its students does not end at graduation. If anything, that is when it truly begins. You carry BGS with you — in how you handle pressure, in how you treat people, in how you never stop learning.

To our current students: you are standing on the shoulders of giants. To our alumni: thank you for coming back. Thank you for not forgetting. The world needs what only a BGS education can produce — people of integrity, courage, and purpose.

God bless you all, and God bless Blessing Group of Schools.

Mrs Marian Ofumaduadike — Principal, Blessing Group of Schools.
